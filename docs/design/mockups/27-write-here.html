Write here

Step 27 proposal: Paste snippets and Write it for here become one action

One empty-bar action that writes at the caret from everything you've saved (snippets, memories, people, tasks, and events) in your voice, the way Reply and Ask already do. The plain snippet list stays inside it as a view, and it's what you get with no model. All people, companies, and text below are made up.

Two paste actions become one. That frees a slot, so in a reply box Reply and Write here both fit on the bar instead of pasting hiding under More.

7. One style that learns from both

Reply and Write here feed one store of what you like, kept per place: email, messaging, work chat, documents. A habit seen in two or more places becomes "everywhere"; one seen in only one place stays there, so a Word doc never picks up chat habits.

What each thing you do teaches

EditYou change the text before Pastemeasured traits (length, capitals, dashes…) at weight 2, as Reply's edits; 15%+ changed → voice sample for this place
AskShorter, Longer, More casual, More formala vote for that side, weight 1, like a pick in Reply
Tell"no em dashes", "always add the ticket link"kept with the place and person; the style-notes call turns repeats into notes about how you write and what you include
SourcesYou drop memories, keep tasksa kind dropped in 3 of your last 5 writes in a place starts left out there (one click puts it back)
TicksSnippets you tick or untickcollections you keep for a place rank higher there
PastePasted as writtenteaches nothing about voice (it's the model's text, decision 27), but counts as the asks and sources being right
