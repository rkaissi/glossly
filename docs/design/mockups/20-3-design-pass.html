Motion

Springs instead of plain curves: things that appear overshoot a little (3 to 8%) and settle, small things squash before they pop, contents follow one by one, and exits ease in fast with no bounce. The springs are real physics turned into CSS linear() curves, so they cost nothing at run time. Windows' "Animation effects" off turns them all off.
